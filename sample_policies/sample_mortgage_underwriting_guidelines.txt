SAMPLE / DEMONSTRATION POLICY
Mortgage Underwriting Guidelines
Version: 2026.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01

DEBT-TO-INCOME REQUIREMENTS

Applications with a calculated debt-to-income ratio above 43% require additional human review.
This demonstration rule is not a lender regulation and does not automatically reject an application.

LOAN-TO-VALUE REQUIREMENTS

A loan-to-value ratio at or below 80% is within this sample threshold.
Ratios above 80% require additional human review and verification of the property valuation.

INCOME VERIFICATION

Reviewers should compare the stated monthly gross income with reliable income documents.
Differences between the application and documents should be investigated before a final decision.

DOCUMENT REVIEW

The reviewer should verify original documents, dates, identity information, and any unclear or conflicting values.
